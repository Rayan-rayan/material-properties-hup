About the data. Values are typical room-temperature figures compiled from standard references (ASM Handbook, MatWeb, Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design and ASTM specifications). Real values depend on product form, section size, heat treatment and supplier. Stress–strain curves are idealized from the tabulated values. Check the mill certificate or a design standard before final design.
