About Material Properties Hub

Material Properties Hub is a free reference for students, engineers and researchers who need the mechanical properties of common metals quickly: yield strength, tensile strength, elastic modulus, elongation, hardness, density and thermal properties.

The site was started by a mechanical engineering student who wanted one clean place to look up the numbers used in materials, mechanics and design courses, together with tools that show how those numbers are used.

Where the data comes from

Values are typical room-temperature figures compiled from widely used references: the ASM Handbook, MatWeb, Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design and the relevant ASTM and SAE specifications. Each material lists its condition (temper or heat treatment) because properties change a lot with processing.

How the stress–strain curves are made

Curves are idealized. For ductile metals the site fits a Ramberg–Osgood curve through the tabulated modulus, 0.2% offset yield strength and tensile strength, then adds a necking region to the listed elongation. They show the shape and key points of a tensile test; they are not measured test records.

Use with care

Design with minimum values from the governing specification or the supplier's certificate, not with typical values. Report errors through the contact page and they will be corrected.
