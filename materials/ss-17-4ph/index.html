Stainless Steel 17-4 PH properties

Condition H1025UNS S17400Family: Stainless steel

Stainless Steel 17-4 PH (Condition H1025) has a typical yield strength of 1,000 MPa (145.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 1,070 MPa (155.2 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 196 GPa (28.4 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 7.78 g/cm³ (0.281 lb/in³).
