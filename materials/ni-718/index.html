Inconel 718 properties

Solution treated and agedUNS N07718Family: Nickel alloy

Inconel 718 (Solution treated and aged) has a typical yield strength of 1,035 MPa (150.1 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 1,240 MPa (179.8 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 200 GPa (29.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 8.19 g/cm³ (0.296 lb/in³).
