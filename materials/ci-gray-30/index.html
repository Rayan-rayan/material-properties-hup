Gray Cast Iron Class 30 properties

ASTM A48, as castUNS F12401Family: Cast iron

Gray Cast Iron Class 30 (ASTM A48, as cast) has no distinct yield point, an ultimate tensile strength of 214 MPa (31.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 100 GPa (14.5 Msi). Elongation at break is about 0.6% and density is 7.15 g/cm³ (0.258 lb/in³).

Common questions

What is the yield strength of Gray Cast Iron Class 30 ASTM A48, as cast?

It has no distinct yield point. Designers use the tensile strength, 214 MPa (31.0 ksi), with a larger factor of safety.

What is the tensile strength of Gray Cast Iron Class 30 ASTM A48, as cast?

About 214 MPa (31.0 ksi).

What is the Young's modulus of Gray Cast Iron Class 30 ASTM A48, as cast?

About 100 GPa (14.5 Msi), with a shear modulus of about 39.7 GPa.
