Titanium Grade 2 properties

Commercially pure, annealedUNS R50400Family: Titanium

Titanium Grade 2 (Commercially pure, annealed) has a typical yield strength of 275 MPa (39.9 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 345 MPa (50.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 105 GPa (15.2 Msi). Elongation at break is about 20% and density is 4.51 g/cm³ (0.163 lb/in³).
