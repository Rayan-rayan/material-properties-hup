AISI 1045 Steel properties

Cold drawnUNS G10450Family: Carbon steel

AISI 1045 Steel (Cold drawn) has a typical yield strength of 530 MPa (76.9 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 625 MPa (90.6 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 7.87 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
