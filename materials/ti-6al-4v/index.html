Titanium Ti-6Al-4V properties

Grade 5, annealedUNS R56400Family: Titanium

Titanium Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5, annealed) has a typical yield strength of 880 MPa (127.6 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 950 MPa (137.8 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 114 GPa (16.5 Msi). Elongation at break is about 14% and density is 4.43 g/cm³ (0.160 lb/in³).
