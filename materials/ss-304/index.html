Stainless Steel 304 properties

AnnealedUNS S30400Family: Stainless steel

Stainless Steel 304 (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 215 MPa (31.2 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 505 MPa (73.2 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 193 GPa (28.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 70% and density is 8.00 g/cm³ (0.289 lb/in³).
