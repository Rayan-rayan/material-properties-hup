Stainless Steel 410 properties

AnnealedUNS S41000Family: Stainless steel

Stainless Steel 410 (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 275 MPa (39.9 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 485 MPa (70.3 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 200 GPa (29.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 20% and density is 7.74 g/cm³ (0.280 lb/in³).

Cutlery, valve trim, pump shafts and steam turbine parts.
