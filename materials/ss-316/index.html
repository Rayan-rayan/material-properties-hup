Stainless Steel 316 properties

AnnealedUNS S31600Family: Stainless steel

Stainless Steel 316 (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 205 MPa (29.7 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 515 MPa (74.7 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 193 GPa (28.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 60% and density is 8.00 g/cm³ (0.289 lb/in³).
