AISI 1095 Steel properties

AnnealedUNS G10950Family: Carbon steel

AISI 1095 Steel (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 380 MPa (55.1 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 655 MPa (95.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 13% and density is 7.85 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
