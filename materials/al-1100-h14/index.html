Aluminum 1100 properties

H14 temperUNS A91100Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 1100 (H14) has a typical yield strength of 115 MPa (16.7 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 125 MPa (18.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 69.0 GPa (10.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 9% and density is 2.71 g/cm³ (0.098 lb/in³).
