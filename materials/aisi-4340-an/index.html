AISI 4340 Steel properties

AnnealedUNS G43400Family: Alloy steel

AISI 4340 Steel (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 470 MPa (68.2 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 745 MPa (108.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 22% and density is 7.85 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
