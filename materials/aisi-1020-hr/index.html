AISI 1020 Steel properties

Hot rolledUNS G10200Family: Carbon steel

AISI 1020 Steel (Hot rolled) has a typical yield strength of 210 MPa (30.5 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 380 MPa (55.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 200 GPa (29.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 25% and density is 7.87 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
