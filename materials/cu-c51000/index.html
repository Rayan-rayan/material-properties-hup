Phosphor Bronze C51000 properties

Hard (H04)UNS C51000Family: Copper alloy

Phosphor Bronze C51000 (Hard (H04)) has a typical yield strength of 515 MPa (74.7 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 560 MPa (81.2 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 110 GPa (16.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 10% and density is 8.86 g/cm³ (0.320 lb/in³).
