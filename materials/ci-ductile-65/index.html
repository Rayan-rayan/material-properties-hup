Ductile Iron 65-45-12 properties

ASTM A536UNS F33100Family: Cast iron

Ductile Iron 65-45-12 (ASTM A536) has a typical yield strength of 310 MPa (45.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 448 MPa (65.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 169 GPa (24.5 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 7.10 g/cm³ (0.257 lb/in³).
