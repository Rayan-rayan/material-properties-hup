AISI 1018 Steel properties

Cold drawnUNS G10180Family: Carbon steel

AISI 1018 Steel (Cold drawn) has a typical yield strength of 370 MPa (53.7 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 440 MPa (63.8 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 15% and density is 7.87 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
