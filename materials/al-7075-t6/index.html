Aluminum 7075 properties

T6 temperUNS A97075Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 7075 (T6) has a typical yield strength of 503 MPa (73.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 572 MPa (83.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 71.7 GPa (10.4 Msi). Elongation at break is about 11% and density is 2.81 g/cm³ (0.102 lb/in³).
