Magnesium AZ31B properties

H24 sheetUNS M11311Family: Magnesium

Magnesium AZ31B (H24 sheet) has a typical yield strength of 220 MPa (31.9 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 290 MPa (42.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 45.0 GPa (6.5 Msi). Elongation at break is about 15% and density is 1.77 g/cm³ (0.064 lb/in³).
