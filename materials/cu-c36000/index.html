Free-Cutting Brass C36000 properties

Half hard (H02)UNS C36000Family: Copper alloy

Free-Cutting Brass C36000 (Half hard (H02)) has a typical yield strength of 310 MPa (45.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 385 MPa (55.8 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 97.0 GPa (14.1 Msi). Elongation at break is about 25% and density is 8.50 g/cm³ (0.307 lb/in³).
