Aluminum 5052 properties

H32 temperUNS A95052Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 5052 (H32) has a typical yield strength of 193 MPa (28.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 228 MPa (33.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 70.3 GPa (10.2 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 2.68 g/cm³ (0.097 lb/in³).
