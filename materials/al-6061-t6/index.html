Aluminum 6061 properties

T6 temperUNS A96061Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 6061 (T6) has a typical yield strength of 276 MPa (40.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 310 MPa (45.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 68.9 GPa (10.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 2.70 g/cm³ (0.098 lb/in³).
