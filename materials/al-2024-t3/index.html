Aluminum 2024 properties

T3 temperUNS A92024Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 2024 (T3) has a typical yield strength of 345 MPa (50.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 483 MPa (70.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 73.1 GPa (10.6 Msi). Elongation at break is about 18% and density is 2.78 g/cm³ (0.100 lb/in³).
