AISI 4140 Steel properties

AnnealedUNS G41400Family: Alloy steel

AISI 4140 Steel (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 415 MPa (60.2 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 655 MPa (95.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 26% and density is 7.85 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
