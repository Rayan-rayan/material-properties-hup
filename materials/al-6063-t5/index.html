Aluminum 6063 properties

T5 temperUNS A96063Family: Aluminum

Aluminum 6063 (T5) has a typical yield strength of 145 MPa (21.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 186 MPa (27.0 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 68.9 GPa (10.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 12% and density is 2.70 g/cm³ (0.098 lb/in³).
