Cartridge Brass C26000 properties

AnnealedUNS C26000Family: Copper alloy

Cartridge Brass C26000 (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 105 MPa (15.2 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 325 MPa (47.1 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 110 GPa (16.0 Msi). Elongation at break is about 62% and density is 8.53 g/cm³ (0.308 lb/in³).
