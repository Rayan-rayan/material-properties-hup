AISI 4140 Steel properties

Quenched and tempered at 425 °CUNS G41400Family: Alloy steel

AISI 4140 Steel (Q&T 425 °C) has a typical yield strength of 1,140 MPa (165.3 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 1,250 MPa (181.3 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 205 GPa (29.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 13% and density is 7.85 g/cm³ (0.284 lb/in³).
