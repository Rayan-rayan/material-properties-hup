Copper C11000 (ETP) properties

AnnealedUNS C11000Family: Copper alloy

Copper C11000 (ETP) (Annealed) has a typical yield strength of 69 MPa (10.0 ksi), an ultimate tensile strength of 220 MPa (31.9 ksi) and a modulus of elasticity of 115 GPa (16.7 Msi). Elongation at break is about 50% and density is 8.89 g/cm³ (0.321 lb/in³).
